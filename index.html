DrugInfo v10 · +WIKIPEDIA

DrugInfo is a free, ad-free drug information search engine. Type any brand, generic or trade name to pull its full profile together in one page: the FDA-approved label, indications, dosage and administration, boxed warnings, contraindications, adverse reactions and drug interactions, plus recalls, shortages, patent status and registered products, and the matching Wikipedia and Wikidata entries with licensed images.

Profiles are compiled live from 21 trusted sources — openFDA, RxNorm, PubChem, DailyMed, Drugs@FDA, FAERS, WHO INN, ClinicalTrials.gov, MedlinePlus, Wikipedia, Wikidata, Wikimedia Commons and more — so you always see the most recent regulatory data instead of a cached copy. Also included: pill identification by imprint, side-by-side drug comparison, an interaction checker, and a global availability view.

Educational reference only — not medical advice. Always confirm dosing, interactions and warnings with a licensed healthcare professional or pharmacist.
